Delete your X Design AI account

X Design AI is developed by ExoCloud.xyz. This page explains how to request deletion of your X Design AI account and the data associated with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to admin@exocloud.xyz with the subject "Delete my X Design AI account".
  2. Include the email address you use to sign in to X Design AI and whether you sign in with Google or Apple. Send the request from that email address if possible, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We confirm receipt and delete the account and the data listed below within 30 days. You will receive an email when deletion is complete.
Email a deletion request

What is deleted

  • Your X Design AI account and sign-in record
  • Your name, email address and profile photo
  • Photos you uploaded, your text prompts and all generated designs (your design history)
  • Your subscription status and purchase records stored by us
  • Analytics data linked to your account

What is kept, and for how long

  • Crash reports do not contain your name or email and are deleted automatically within 90 days.
  • Payment records are held by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies. We do not store your payment card details.

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions) or in your Apple ID settings. Questions: admin@exocloud.xyz.
